## Figure 1 — PRISMA 2020 Flow Diagram

Shows the complete study selection process: from 399 initial records identified in Scopus to 24 studies included in the final synthesis.

**Reference in manuscript:** Figure 1.

## Figure 2 — Ontogenetic Succession of Mucosal Immunity

Timeline figure depicting three developmental phases (lecithotrophic, live feed transition, post-flexion) and the coupled dynamics of feed type, microbial succession, and mucosal effector expression.

**Reference in manuscript:** Figure 2 (Discussion 4.2).

## Graphical Abstract

Visual summary of the review's main findings, integrating the Digestive Immunity Hypothesis with habitat-driven AMP diversification.
