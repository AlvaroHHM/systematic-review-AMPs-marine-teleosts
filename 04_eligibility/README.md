# 04. Eligibility (Full-Text Assessment)

28 studies (2 INCLUDE + 26 MAYBE) were assessed at the full-text stage.

4 studies were excluded for failing the C2 criterion (transcriptomic data required):

- Tessera et al. (2012) - in silico EST mining
- Asensio-Calavia et al. (2026) - comparative genomics
- Miccoli et al. (2021) - qPCR and IHC, no transcriptomics
- Wang et al. (2024) - targeted cloning and ChIRP assays, no RNA-Seq

**Final included studies: 24.**
