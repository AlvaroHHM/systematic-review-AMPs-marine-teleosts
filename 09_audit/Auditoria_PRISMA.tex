\section{Identified Discrepancies and Resolutions}

\subsection{Discrepancy 1: Exclusion Count (263 vs. 264)}

\textbf{Resolution:} The actual count of EXCLUDE records was \textbf{263}, not 264.

\subsection{Discrepancy 2: E6 Category Over-Count (44 vs. 43)}

\textbf{Resolution:} Traced to a single record (Xue et al., 2026) that was classified as MAYBE but had a residual E6 tag. Verified count: 43.

\subsection{Discrepancy 3: Full-Text Exclusions (2 vs. 4)}

\textbf{Resolution:} Additional exclusions identified: Miccoli et al. (2021) and Wang et al. (2024) failed C2. Total: 4.

\subsection{Discrepancy 4: Final Included Studies (26 vs. 24)}

\textbf{Resolution:} $28 - 4 = 24$ studies.

\subsection{Discrepancy 5: De-Duplication Criterion (287 vs. 291 vs. 294)}

\textbf{Resolution:} Union-find over (exact DOI) $\cup$ (fuzzy title $\geq$ 78\%) reproduces 291 exactly.

\subsection{Discrepancy 6: Risk of Bias (17/5/2)}

\textbf{Resolution:} Direct count from Table S2 after removing Miccoli and Wang confirmed 17 Low / 5 Minor / 2 Moderate.
